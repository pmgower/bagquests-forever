## 0.2.0

- 7 more bag questlines (9 total), plus the Alliance route for the Cozy Sleeping Bag
- New tracker window: bordered, movable, resizable, with scroll bars and 4 color schemes
- Bag slot counts, progress dots, and level hints colored like the game's quest colors
- Hover tooltips with click hints; right-click skips a step, shift-right-click undoes it
- Settings page, minimap button, and dim pins for later steps
- Alts table in the same resizable window
- Easy bug reports: /bq feedback, a settings button, ctrl-click on the minimap button, and an offer when BagQuests hits an error
